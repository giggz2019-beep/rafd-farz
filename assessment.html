AI Engineer — Build an intelligent customer-service agent

This is a practical design exercise, not a quiz. There are no multiple-choice questions and no single correct answer. You will design a real AI system for a real business problem, and you will be assessed on your engineering reasoning and technical depth.

Format

The Challenge

A retail client wants RAFD to build an intelligent customer-service agent.

Read the brief, then design the solution across the sections that follow.

What the client already has
  • Product catalog
  • Product prices
  • Product stock information
  • FAQ and company policies
  • Customer orders
  • WhatsApp as the customer communication channel
What the agent must do
  • Understand customer questions.
  • Answer using the company knowledge base.
  • Search product information.
  • Check order status through an API/tool.
  • Check stock availability.
  • Avoid hallucinating information.
  • Escalate when it lacks enough information.
  • Maintain conversation context.
  • Be designed to connect to WhatsApp later.
  • Be deployable securely for a real business.
ⓘ Write for a senior engineer who will read this after you leave. Specifics beat volume — a short precise answer scores better than a long vague one.
